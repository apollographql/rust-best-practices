# Collaboration

Human steering when a reply is possible. Headless = no human turn expected (CI, batch, or instructed); no extra authority.

Stop and present options before:

- hard-to-reverse contracts: public API, wire/schema format, persisted data, config names;
- ownership/concurrency-model or crate-boundary changes;
- dependencies outside the workspace's existing stack;
- ≥2 viable approaches with non-dominated tradeoffs.

Pareto frontier: name objectives/constraints, discard dominated options, expose remaining tradeoffs; recommend one. Ask high-level questions in waves, prioritizing answers that could change the choice (value of information). Otherwise proceed; execute agreed work autonomously, reopen on material evidence.

Headless: choose the most reversible viable option within authority; report choice and alternatives.
